The introduction

A little more about me.

My career began in software development, where I learned to approach problems through code, thoughtful design, and practical solutions. That technical foundation later shaped how I approached the delivery of banking technology projects.

At AlphaSoft K60, I worked as a Software Developer. I then moved into an IT Project Manager role at Aman Bank for Commerce & Investment, collaborating with technical teams, stakeholders, and vendors on digital banking initiatives.

What I enjoy most is connecting the details of engineering with the bigger picture: understanding the problem, aligning people, and helping teams deliver technology that makes sense for its users.
